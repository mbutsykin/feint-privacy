Feint — Privacy Policy & Terms

Last updated: November 14, 2026. Developer: M20 Studios.

Feint is a single-player game for Android. It does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email, contacts, location or other personal information. This policy explains what data is processed when you play and why.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (heroes, items, currencies, settings) is saved only in the app's private storage on your device. We do not have access to it. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Data processed by third-party services

Feint uses the following Google services. They may collect device identifiers and technical data as described below and in Google's own policies.

  • Google Firebase Analytics — anonymous gameplay events (for example: a battle or dungeon started or finished, tutorial progress, a level reached, a store opened, a purchase or ad reward completed) together with device model, OS version, app version, approximate country and an app instance ID. We use this to understand how the game is played and to improve balance and stability. No names, emails or chat content are sent.
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics — crash reports and error logs (stack trace, device model, OS and app version, installation ID) so we can find and fix bugs.
  • Google AdMob — optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch for an in-game reward. AdMob may use the Android advertising ID, IP address and device information to serve and measure ads and, where you allow it, to personalise them.
  • Google Play Billing — optional in-app purchases. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play; we never see your payment details. We receive only the purchase confirmation needed to deliver the item.

Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices

  • Ad consent. In the EEA, the UK and other regions where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent before showing ads. You can change or withdraw it at any time in the game: Settings → Privacy choices.
  • Advertising ID. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Deleting data. Uninstalling the game removes all progress stored on your device. To request deletion of analytics or crash data linked to your app instance, email us (see Contact). Analytics data is otherwise kept for at most 14 months.

Data sharing and sale

We do not sell your data. Data is shared only with the Google services listed above, for the purposes listed above. All data is transmitted over encrypted connections.

In-app purchase terms

  • The game is free to play. Optional purchases (shards, gold bundles, Supporter pack, cosmetics) are processed by Google Play; prices are shown in your local currency before you confirm.
  • Purchased items are virtual game content with no cash value. They cannot be exchanged for money or transferred to another account.
  • Progress, including consumed purchases, is stored only on your device. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data removes it; we cannot restore it. Permanent purchases (such as the Supporter pack) can be restored with "Restore purchases" in the Shards store.
  • Refunds follow Google Play's refund policy: support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637.
  • Where an upgrade has a random outcome (Masterwork, Enchant), the chances are shown in the game before you spend anything.

Children

Feint is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has used the game and data was collected, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
